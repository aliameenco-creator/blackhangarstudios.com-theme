Welcome to Black Hangar Studios

At Black Hangar, we offer a complete, one-stop production experience for film, television, and commercial projects of every scale. Located just 45 minutes from West London and Heathrow Airport, our studio combines the serenity of the countryside with the convenience of city access. Whether you are shooting a feature film, a streaming series, or high-end advertising content, our facilities are designed to provide the space, technology, and creative environment needed to bring your vision to life.

01

A Space Built for Creativity

Our main stage is one of the largest uninterrupted sound stages in the United Kingdom, spanning an impressive 32,000 square feet of open, flexible space. Designed to accommodate even the most ambitious productions, it provides a blank canvas for everything from large-scale set builds to complex special effects. In addition, we are proud to house the UK’s largest permanent green screen cyclorama, offering filmmakers and content creators an unparalleled opportunity for immersive virtual production and visual effects work.

02

Full Production Facilities on Site

The studio is a fully self-contained lot with everything required to support your entire production pipeline from concept to completion. Alongside our main stage, we provide a range of large workshops for set construction and prop fabrication, spacious production offices for creative and logistical teams, and dedicated post-production facilities equipped for editing, sound, and finishing. Every aspect of the studio has been designed with efficiency and flexibility in mind, allowing productions to work seamlessly without the need to travel between multiple locations.

03

Designed for Convenience

Accessibility and practicality are at the core of what we offer. Our location benefits from excellent road and rail links, making it easy for crews, cast, and suppliers to reach us from London, Heathrow, and beyond. On site, our expansive studio back lot provides additional space for exterior set builds, extra office units, parking, and facility vehicles. This layout ensures that productions of any size can operate smoothly, with all departments and resources close at hand.

04

A Home for Every Production

From independent filmmakers to international studios, Black Hangar welcomes productions of all kinds. Our experienced team is committed to providing a professional, supportive, and creative environment that helps bring every project to its fullest potential. Whether you’re building entire worlds on our stages or capturing the beauty of natural light on our back lot, we provide the infrastructure and expertise to make it happen.

What types of productions can use Black Hangar?

The studio welcomes film, television, commercial and music-video productions, from independent projects to international studio productions.

How large is the main sound stage?

The main stage offers 32,000 square feet of open, flexible space. Contact the team to discuss set requirements, technical specifications and availability.

Are production and post-production facilities on site?

Workshops, production offices and post-production facilities support set construction, editing, sound and finishing. Discuss your specific requirements with the studio team.

Where is the studio located?

Black Hangar Studios is in Hampshire. The existing website describes it as approximately 45 minutes from West London and Heathrow Airport; actual journey times depend on route and traffic.

How do I enquire about availability?

Contact the team with your proposed dates, production type and space requirements. They can discuss availability and a quotation for your project.
