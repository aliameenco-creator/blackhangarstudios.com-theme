What do you have available at the studio for hire?

We have a wide range of services available at the studio including production offices, Hair & Make-Up, Green Rooms, Workshops, Catering rooms, Parking, back lots, screening rooms, location services, Studio space, and financing.

How much does it cost to rent the space?

To rent just the studio space per day is £5500.00. However, should you wish to rent other rooms in the office space, or the photogrammetry studio, they have a separate per day cost.

How can I get to the studio?

We are a 45 minute drive from West London and Heathrow. However, if you are coming from central London, you can take a train to our local village, Alton, which is 10 minutes away, or Basingstoke, which is 20 minutes away.

Who has hired your space before?

Our studio has seen many uses over the last two decades, including indie filmmakers, all the way up to Conglomerate Studios including Disney, Netflix, Amazon Studios, BBC, and AMC.

Can I hire the studio for event space?

Yes! We have hosted many large scale events at the studio, including concerts, weddings and car events.

Do you have accommodation on-site for crew?

We have trailers, cabins and caravans on-site that you would be able to add to your hire.

Are there any images of the studio online?

We have a virtual studio tour available online that you can view.

I am a freelancer, when can I expect payment for services?

Our finance team will always pay your invoices based on the agreed payment terms in your contract. If you are experiencing issues, please contact admin@blackhangarstudios.com
