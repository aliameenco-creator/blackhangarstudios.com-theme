Testing on-site and Mobile Available

01

ICGENE LAMP TEST

LAMP Testing uses six points of attachment in the genetic material, In comparison to other testing methods, which only rely on two points. The Enbiotech kit allows us to perform a rapid and specific qualitative test of SARS-CoV-2 in clinical swabs using a “Point of care” system and allowing to perform up to 12 tests simultaneously. The kit was tested for the detection of two gene markers.

02

PROMATE PCR TEST

We are now offering non-invasive PCR testing. We test in blocks of 30 people, producing results within 60 minutes. Our workflow is a combination of a direct to PCR sample processing method and molecular in vitro diagnostic test for the detection of the SARS-CoV-2 RNA from anterior nasal dry swabs for interpretation on the genesig® q16 and q32.

03

Fit to Fly Certificates

Just 45 minutes from London Heathrow. Some countries require passengers to show proof of a negative, COVID-19 test within certain timeframes of departure. We are able to offer on-site and mobile testing with Fit to fly certificates for crew and talent. Results available in under 60 minutes.
